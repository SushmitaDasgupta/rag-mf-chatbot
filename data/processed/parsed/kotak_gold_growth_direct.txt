## About
Kotak Gold Fund Growth - Direct is a hybrid fund. This fund was started on 1 January, 2013. The fund is managed by Abhishek Bisen, Jeetu Sonar. This fund gives you the benefit of diversification.

## Key Parameters
Kotak Gold Fund has ₹6532 Cr worth of assets under management (AUM) as on Aug 2026 and is more than category average. The fund has an expense ratio 0.7.

## Returns

## Taxation
As it is a sector - precious metals mutual fund the taxation is as follows:If the fund is equity oriented i.e. asset allocation of more than 65% in equity instruments:For short term (less than a year) capital gains will be taxed at 20%For long term (more than 1 year) capital gains will be taxed at 12.5% without indexation benefitLong term gains up to Rs 1 lakh are exempt from taxation (more than 12 months).
